## The post

Everyone has heard that compound interest is powerful.

→ 10,000 pesos at 10% for 20 years: simple vs. compound: 30,000 vs 67,000

→ With compound interest you earn 1,000 the first year, then 1,100 the second, then 1,210 the third — because now you're earning on 11,000, then on 12,100.

→ Someone who invests 2,000 pesos a month from age 25 to 35 and then stops completely, contributing nothing for the next thirty years, usually ends up ahead of someone who starts at 35 and contributes every month until 65.
