About

Hi, I'm Jaime.

I'm Jaime Sandoval Ricaño, a student and content creator passionate about finance, business, and entrepreneurship. Through TikTok and LinkedIn, I share financial news, educational content, and insights to make finance easier to understand, especially for young people.

My goal is to keep learning, share valuable knowledge, and help more people become interested in the world of finance.

Through my work in financial education and content creation, I have had the opportunity to speak with leading figures in the financial sector, including Margaret Franklin, CEO of CFA Institute; Jon Maier, Chief ETF Strategist at J.P. Morgan Asset Management; Moris Dieck, one of Mexico's most prominent financial educators; Andy Toh, CEO of Bluesky Education; and many other industry professionals.

Fear index (VIX)

 

Basically, how much fear there is in the market. It measures how big a swing traders expect in the S&P 500 over the next 30 days. Under 20 is calm, over 30 means people are nervous. It usually spikes when stocks fall — which is why it's nicknamed the fear index.
